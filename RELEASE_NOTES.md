Butterfly Speak 0.2.0

The first public release.

- Hold Ctrl+Win, speak and release, and the text is typed into the app that has focus. Double-tap the shortcut for hands-free dictation.
- Three speech engines: Cloud (sign in with Google, 2,000 words a week), Bring your own Sarvam key, and On-device (English only, with models downloaded when you choose one).
- English and 22 Indian languages through Sarvam AI, detected automatically or set to one.
- AI Polish removes fillers, applies self-corrections and fixes grammar, with Sarvam-105B in the cloud modes or a small local model on-device.
- Your own AI endpoint can take over speech-to-text, AI Polish, or both.
- Shortcuts to translate a dictation, ask the voice agent, paste or copy the last transcript, undo an AI edit and run transforms on selected text.
- Dictionary, snippets, and corrections learned from your edits.
- Notes, with import of audio and video recordings for transcription (needs a Sarvam key).
- History and Insights, kept on your computer.
- Delete your Cloud account from Settings → Speech engine.
- Checks for signed updates and installs nothing until you choose to.
